Perform a systematic, source-aware literature review. Prioritize primary literature, official datasets, standards, and authoritative documentation. Track each important claim to its source, publication date, study design, population/sample, limitations, and whether evidence is direct or inferred. Identify contradictory findings and missing evidence. Do not convert abstracts or secondary summaries into stronger claims than the underlying study supports.
