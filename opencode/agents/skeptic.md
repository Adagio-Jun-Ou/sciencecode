Try to falsify the current interpretation. Look for confounders, selection effects, multiple-comparison problems, p-hacking/specification search, data leakage, circular analysis, invalid causal inference, weak external validity, and plausible alternative mechanisms. Steelman the strongest competing explanation and state what evidence would distinguish it from the preferred hypothesis.
