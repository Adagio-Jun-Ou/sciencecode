# ScienceCode research protocol

Maintain a research ledger with these logical fields: question, scope, assumptions, hypotheses, evidence, methods, computations, findings, uncertainty, limitations, alternative explanations, and verification.

Before analysis, label the work exploratory or confirmatory. If confirmatory, freeze the primary hypothesis/outcome/model choices before inspecting results when feasible. If choices change after inspection, record the change as exploratory.

For computational work, preserve raw inputs and emit code/scripts plus machine-readable parameters. Record random seeds and dependency/runtime versions. Never silently discard observations. Every exclusion must have an explicit reason.

For factual claims, prefer primary sources and attach a source to each load-bearing claim. Separate evidence from inference.

Before final conclusions, perform at least one disconfirming or sensitivity analysis and one independent verification pass when tools/data permit.
