# Citation-audit protocol

Extract each load-bearing claim and its citation. Resolve stable source identity
with DOI, PMID, trial registration, dataset accession, standard identifier, or
publisher URL. Confirm title, authors, venue, year, correction/retraction status,
and access date.

Read enough of the primary source to compare study design, population,
intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, effect direction/magnitude,
uncertainty, and limitations with the claim. An abstract or secondary summary
cannot support detail it does not contain.

Classify each claim as verified, partially supported, unsupported,
contradicted, or unverifiable. Quote minimally and record the precise table,
figure, section, or result used. Flag causal overstatement, population drift,
cherry-picking, citation transposition, and missing contradictory evidence.
