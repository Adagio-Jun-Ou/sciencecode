# Meta-analysis protocol

Predefine eligibility, search dates, outcomes, time points, effect measures,
unit of analysis, multi-arm handling, duplicate cohorts, and risk-of-bias
assessment. Keep study selection and extraction auditable.

Do not pool clinically or methodologically incompatible estimates merely
because they share a label. Harmonize effect direction and units explicitly.
Record every conversion and imputation. Choose fixed/random/hierarchical models
from the data-generating assumptions, not the desired significance.

Report study-level effects, pooled effect with uncertainty, heterogeneity,
influence/leave-one-out analysis, sensitivity to model and risk-of-bias
decisions, and small-study/publication-bias diagnostics with their limitations.
Distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
