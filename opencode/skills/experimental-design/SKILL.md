# Experimental-design protocol

Write the question as a target estimand: population, intervention/exposure,
comparison, outcome, and time horizon. Choose a design that can identify that
estimand and list every required assumption.

For experiments, specify randomization unit and method, allocation concealment,
blinding, controls, interference risks, adherence, measurement schedule,
sample-size or precision target, stopping rules, multiplicity handling, and the
primary analysis before outcomes are inspected.

For observational studies, state the causal graph or equivalent assumptions.
Distinguish confounders from mediators and colliders. Address selection,
measurement error, time-varying confounding, immortal-time bias, missingness,
and negative controls. Predefine falsification and sensitivity analyses.

End with the exact conclusions the design can support, the conclusions it
cannot support, and the evidence that would overturn the favored hypothesis.
